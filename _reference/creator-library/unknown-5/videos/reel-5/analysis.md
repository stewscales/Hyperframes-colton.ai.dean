## Caption style — SIGNATURE SYSTEM
This reel's most distinctive feature is its **two-layer caption system**:

**Layer 1 (keyword / emphasis)**:
- Font: Extra-bold condensed sans (similar to Bebas Neue or Impact), possibly custom/outline
- Size: Very large — 80–100px equivalent, nearly full-width
- Color: **Golden yellow** (#FFB800–#FFC83D area) — warm, high-contrast
- Position: Upper-center or center, overlaid on face
- Case: ALL CAPS

**Layer 2 (spoken word)**:
- Font: Medium-weight sans, clean
- Size: ~36–44px
- Color: White
- Position: Below Layer 1, mid-screen
- Case: Mixed case
- Sync: Tight word-by-word

Together they create a "movie poster + subtitle" dual-read — eye catches yellow keyword, then reads white phrase.
