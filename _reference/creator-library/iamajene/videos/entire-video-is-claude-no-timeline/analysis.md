First words: "This entire video that you're seeing right now is edited by Claude." The title holds ~3s then fades as the first prop arrives.

## Pacing
- Total scenes: ~14 visual states in 43s; a new visual event every 1–2.5s even when the shot itself doesn't change
- Average scene length: ~3s; shortest ~1s (list beats), longest ~11s (tier-list split screen, but the graphic changes every 1–2s inside it)
- Distribution feel: medium shots, dense events
- Pace shifts: fastest during the opening list ("the captions, the cuts, the mistakes…"), slowest on the template demo, then quick again for the CTA

## Captions
- Style: phrase chunks of 2–4 words, sentence case, no outline — soft drop shadow only
- Font feel: tight-tracked neo-grotesque (SF Pro-like), medium/semibold, small
- Active-word treatment: the key word of a sentence is **replaced by an oversized italic serif in the accent colour**, overlapping the small caption line ("captions", "my head", "everything.", "templates.", "free", "EDIT")
- Position: chest height (about 58% down), centred; moves to ~27% when the frame is split
- Size relative to frame: small (caption) + huge (accent word)

## Transitions
- Flavors observed: hard cuts between shots; props and cards ease in (short slide/scale + fade); full-screen graphics cut in and out on the sentence boundary
- Rotation cadence: no flashy transitions at all — the motion lives in the props
- Signature: graphic states change exactly on the spoken word

## Face treatment
- Mode mix: fullscreen (most), bottom-half under a graphic (tier list), absent (full-screen graphics, b-roll)
- Grading: dark, warm, soft contrast; background slightly dim so white text reads without an outline
- Camera motion: none noticeable; no punch-ins
- Framing: consistent mid-wide, centred

## Audio reactivity

- Everything is synced to speech, not music

## Signature move
Small quiet captions with one oversized italic-serif accent word per sentence, while a UI prop that literally shows what he is saying appears on the exact word.

## What to steal (actionable for Hyperframes builds)
- Pinned two-line title for the first ~3s, second line in a solid accent bar
- Caption system: small sentence-case sans + one big italic serif accent word per sentence in the single brand accent colour (max ~1 per sentence)
- "Show the noun": every list item gets a purpose-built UI card (timeline strip, checklist, status pill, doc card, notification toast) that changes state on the word
- One or two full-screen dark graphic beats per video (checklist ticking in sync is the strongest)
- Split layout so a diagram can build while he keeps talking
- CTA = keyword as the accent word + notification toasts
- No transitions, no zooms: restraint is part of why it looks expensive
